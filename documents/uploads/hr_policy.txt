
PEOPLESPHERE AI - HR POLICY

1. Annual Leave
Employees are eligible for 20 days of paid annual leave per year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are eligible for 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate may be required for extended sick leave.

3. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

4. Work From Home
Employees can request work from home with prior approval from their manager.

5. Leave Approval
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR system.
Manager approval is required before taking leave.
